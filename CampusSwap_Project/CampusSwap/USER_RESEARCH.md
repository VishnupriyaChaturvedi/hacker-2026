# User Research — Do this before presenting as validated research

The brief says: **Talk to a real user in the first hour.** Do not claim the persona or numbers below are real research. They are prototype assumptions until validated.

## 5-minute interview script
Ask one student who has bought or sold something through a campus WhatsApp group:

1. “Tell me about the last thing you bought or sold through WhatsApp.”
2. “What was the most annoying part?”
3. “How did you find out whether it was still available?”
4. “Did you negotiate the price? How?”
5. “What makes you trust or distrust a student seller?”
6. “If a campus marketplace existed, what one feature would make you use it?”
7. “Would you prefer campus email verification, ratings, or something else?”

## Validation scorecard
After the interview, update the pitch with:
- One exact quote.
- One surprising behavior.
- One thing CampusSwap got wrong.
- One feature to change based on evidence.

## Prototype hypotheses to test
H1: Students want listings separated from chat.
H2: Visible seller reputation increases willingness to contact.
H3: A structured Make Offer button is easier than bargaining in free text.
H4: Public campus meet-up suggestions reduce safety anxiety.
