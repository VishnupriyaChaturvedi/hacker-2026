# 3-Minute Demo Script

### 0:00 — Problem
“Imagine I have a Casio calculator to sell. I post it in my campus WhatsApp group. Two hours later, the post is buried. People ask the same questions and I have no easy way to show that I’m a trustworthy seller.”

### 0:25 — Product
“This is CampusSwap: a campus-only marketplace built around the behaviors students already have — browsing, chatting and bargaining.”

### 0:45 — Buyer journey
Click **Explore deals** → Books → search DSA → open listing.

Say: “Instead of scrolling a chat history, I can see the price, condition, description and verified seller in one card.”

### 1:15 — Bargain
Click **Chat** → enter offer such as ₹220 → **Make offer**.

Say: “Negotiation is structured. The buyer makes an offer, and the seller can accept or counter. No more confusing price messages.”

### 1:45 — Seller journey
Click **Sell something** → enter an item → publish.

Say: “The seller can post once and the listing stays discoverable.”

### 2:15 — Trust
Scroll to Trust.

Say: “For students, the hardest part is not finding a cheap item. It is trusting the person. So the design makes verification, ratings, trade count and safe meet-up spots visible.”

### 2:40 — Close
“WhatsApp created the community. CampusSwap gives that community the marketplace tools it was missing.”
